Projects

Analyzing High-Tech Export Dynamics in the EU (2012–2021) using ML Methods

In this project, key economic and institutional drivers of high-tech exports are identified using panel data with Elastic Net, decision trees, PCA. Clusters of countries are created based on their export profiles.

Forecasting US GDP Growth: Comparing Machine Learning Models to AR(4) Model

This project investigates the predictive capacity of machine learning methods (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and PCA) in comparison to an autoregressive benchmark (AR(4)). SHAP-based feature attribution and density analysis are employed to examine the directional influence of macroeconomic indicators.

View Details
A Comparative Time Series Analysis of Income-Consumption Relationships in the US and Japan (1975–2021)

In this project, the income–consumption relationships in the US and Japan are examined using time series methods such as cointegration analysis, VAR, Granger causality and structural break tests.
